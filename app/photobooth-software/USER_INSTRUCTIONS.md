# SIC Photobooth User Instructions

These instructions begin after the event and operator have been selected. Choose the experience you want to create, then follow the prompts on screen.

## Flipbook

1. Choose **Flipbook** from the experience screen.
2. Pick a frame design, then tap **I LIKE THIS**. If no choice is made, the currently selected frame is used after 60 seconds.
3. Read the quick instructions and tap **LET'S GO**, or wait for the 10-second countdown to begin automatically.
4. Pose for three cover photos. Each photo is taken automatically after a 10-second countdown.
5. Get ready for three video clips. Each clip begins after a 10-second countdown and records for 5 seconds.
6. Choose your favorite cover photo, then tap **I LIKE THIS**. The current selection continues automatically after 60 seconds.
7. Choose your favorite video clip for the flipbook, then tap **I LIKE THIS**. The current selection continues automatically after 60 seconds.
8. Wait while the booth creates your flipbook GIF. If processing fails or takes longer than two minutes, the booth returns to cover-photo capture so you can try again.
9. On the completion screen, scan the QR code to open your digital flipbook. The displayed code can also be used to identify the output.
10. Tap **Print** to open the print handoff. Choose all sheets or one sheet, set the number of copies, then select **Print**. You can also download the print PDF.
11. Tap **Session Done!** when finished. If nothing has been printed, the booth asks whether to print now or exit anyway.

## Photo Strips

1. Choose **Photo Strip** from the experience screen.
2. Select a layout. Use **Portrait** or **Landscape** to filter the options, then tap **I LIKE THIS**. If no choice is made, the selected layout continues after 60 seconds.
3. Check the layout's photo count, then tap **LET'S GO**. The camera also starts automatically after 10 seconds.
4. Pose for each photo. The booth takes photos automatically, one at a time, until all layout slots are filled.
5. On the review screen, tap a photo frame to select it.
6. To replace a photo, tap **Retake photo #** for the selected frame. You can take up to four retakes in total.
7. Retaking replaces the selected photo directly without affecting the other photos. Tap any of the 4 filter presets (Original, Classic B&W, Vintage Sepia, Warm Golden) to style your photos before finishing.
8. Tap **I'm finished** when the strip looks right. The current strip is confirmed automatically after 60 seconds if no action is taken.
9. Scan the QR code on the completion screen to view your digital photo strip. The displayed code can also be used to identify the output.
10. Tap **Print** to open the system print dialog. After printing, record additional copies if required.
11. Tap **Session Done!** when finished. If nothing has been printed, the booth asks whether to print now or exit anyway.
